FAQs

I already have someone editing my videos. Why would I need you?

Because editing isn’t the bottleneck. You could have the best-edited video in your market and still have no owner call you, because nobody found it and nobody remembered it. Your editor is responsible for how it looks. We’re responsible for whether the right people see it: search, packaging, distribution, and staying visible over the months it takes an owner to decide to sell. Most of our clients keep their editor. We’re a different line item.

What if I already have a marketing team?

Then use them for what they’re good at: website, email, event logistics. Very few in-house marketing teams have built a search and content distribution system aimed at business owners considering a sale. That’s a specific skill set, not a general marketing function. We plug into what they’re already doing rather than replace it.

Why isn’t posting on LinkedIn enough?

Because posting isn’t the same as being found. LinkedIn reaches people who already follow you. It doesn’t reach the owner who’s never heard of you and is searching “sell my business” or “business valuation” at 11pm. That’s a search and content problem, not a posting problem, and it’s most of the reason a good post gets forty likes and zero calls.

How long does it usually take before this starts generating opportunities?

Visibility moves first: search presence, reach, and packaging typically shift inside 30 to 60 days. Deal flow moves slower, because most owners take 6 to 18 months to decide to sell after they first come across you. We’re not chasing a fast win; we’re making sure you’re the name they already know when that decision happens.

What actually happens after I become a client?

You record. We handle the rest: editing, clips, titles, thumbnails, publishing schedule, and search optimization across every platform it goes to. You get one call a month to review what’s working and what we’re changing. If you’re spending time managing us day to day, we’ve done our job wrong.

How do you measure whether this is working?

Reach and search visibility, because those move first and tell us the packaging is right. Inbound conversations, because that’s the leading indicator of deal flow. And actual deal flow, tracked over the longer window it realistically takes. We report all three every month, not just the numbers that are easy to show.

Why wouldn’t I just hire another freelancer or agency?

Most freelancers handle production. Most agencies handle campaigns with a start and an end date. Neither is built for a buyer group that takes 6 to 18 months to decide. We’re built specifically for that timeline and this audience: search, packaging, and distribution aimed at owners and their advisors, running continuously instead of in bursts.

I don’t have a large following. Does this even work for me?

It’s not built to work off an existing following. The audience you need: owners thinking about an exit, and the CPAs, attorneys, and bankers around them, mostly isn’t following anyone in this space yet. We build reach through search and targeted distribution, not by hoping your existing list converts.
